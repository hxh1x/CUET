# NFSU GANDHINAGAR — M.SC. CYBER SECURITY ADMISSION & ELIGIBILITY GUIDE
**Target Institution:** National Forensic Sciences University (NFSU), Gandhinagar Campus  
**School:** School of Cyber Security and Digital Forensics (SCSDF)  
**Program:** Master of Science in Cyber Security (M.Sc. Cyber Security)  
**Entrance Test Code:** CUET-PG SCQP09  
**Aspiration:** All India Rank 1 (AIR 1)

---

## 1. Institutional Context & University Profile
The **National Forensic Sciences University (NFSU)** is an Institution of National Importance (INI) established by an Act of Parliament (Act 32 of 2020) under the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India. 
The Gandhinagar campus is the flagship headquarters and houses state-of-the-art cyber defense centers, malware analysis labs, SCADA/ICS security testbeds, and digital forensic research facilities.

---

## 2. Program Details: M.Sc. Cyber Security
- **Degree Awarded:** Master of Science in Cyber Security
- **Duration:** 2 Academic Years (4 Semesters)
- **Host School:** School of Cyber Security & Digital Forensics (SCSDF)
- **Key Specialization Tracks:**
  - Network Security & Cryptographic Protocols
  - Digital Forensics & Incident Response (DFIR)
  - Malware Analysis & Reverse Engineering
  - Cloud Security, Threat Intelligence & Vulnerability Management
  - Critical Infrastructure & Cyber Warfare Defense

---

## 3. Official Academic Eligibility Criteria

### A. Qualifying Degrees
Candidates must have completed or be appearing in the final year of any of the following degrees from a recognized university:
1. **B.E. / B.Tech.** in Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) / Information Technology (IT) / Computer Engineering (CE) / Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE) / Information and Communication Technology (ICT)
2. **Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)**
3. **B.Sc.** in Information Technology / Computer Science / Electronics
4. Equivalent undergraduate technical qualification recognized by the University / UGC / AICTE.

### B. Minimum Aggregate Marks in Qualifying Examination
- **General / OBC (NCL) / General-EWS:** Minimum **55% aggregate marks** or equivalent CGPA (e.g., 6.0/10.0 scale).
- **SC / ST / PwD (Persons with Benchmark Disabilities):** Minimum **50% aggregate marks** or equivalent CGPA (e.g., 5.5/10.0 scale).

---

## 4. Admission Architecture & Selection Process



1. **CUET-PG Requirement:** NFSU mandates scores from **CUET-PG Paper Code SCQP09** for admissions into M.Sc. Cyber Security.
2. **Merit Determination:** Merit is computed strictly on the basis of the score obtained in CUET-PG SCQP09.
3. **Seat Matrix & Reservation:** Admissions adhere to Government of India reservation mandates (OBC-NCL: 27%, SC: 15%, ST: 7.5%, EWS: 10%, PwD horizontal: 5%).
4. **AIR 1 Strategic Advantage:** An AIR 1 ranking secures immediate first-round selection for the Gandhinagar campus, choice of specialized cyber defense electives, and prioritized research lab allocations.
