# CUET-PG SCQP09 — final preparation package

**Start with:** `01_MASTER_SYLLABUS/SCQP09_Master_Syllabus_2024-2026.pdf`

A 50-page, learning-ordered syllabus and preparation plan built from all **225 questions** in the official NTA papers of **2024, 2025 and 2026**. The 2022 and 2023 papers are deliberately excluded because the pattern changed.

## What is inside

| Folder | What you need it for |
|---|---|
| `01_MASTER_SYLLABUS/` | The main 50-page PDF, its editable HTML source, and the 92-module taxonomy CSV. |
| `02_QUESTION_BANK/` | All 225 classified questions as CSV and JSON. Filter by topic or subtopic and drill. |
| `03_ORIGINAL_PAPERS/` | The three official NTA papers used as evidence. |
| `04_REFERENCE/` | NFSU Gandhinagar M.Sc. Cyber Security eligibility note. |
| `05_SOURCE/` | Build and classification scripts, kept for provenance and reproducibility. |

## The short version of the analysis

- **Mathematics:** 70/225 (31.1%) — the largest block. 2024 had 30 questions; 2025 and 2026 had 20 each.
- **Thinking & Decision Making:** 50/225 (22.2%) — the fastest-scoring block and a daily habit, not a final chapter.
- **DSA + Digital + OS:** 104/225 (46.2%) — the stable CS core.
- **DBMS/CN:** 1 DBMS question and 0 CN questions in three years. Keep them as a two-hour insurance plan, not a full subject.
- **Format:** 2025 and 2026 were 90-minute papers, so train at roughly **72 seconds per question**. 2026 increased match-the-list, arrange-the-order and assertion–reason questions.

## Suggested order of use

1. Read the exam-pattern and learning-order sections of the PDF.
2. Follow the 92 module cards in order; keep TDM as a daily 20–30 minute track.
3. Use the question bank CSV to drill one subtopic at a time.
4. Finish with the 16-week plan, the final 10-day checklist and the formula sheet.

The older synthetic mock/stub material has been removed. This package contains only the evidence-based syllabus, the real question bank and the source papers.
